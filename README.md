# Aim

To demonstrate the ability of text-to-video generation tools to reproduce an existing visual scene by crafting precise and progressively refined prompts. The objective is to identify important visual elements such as objects, motion, camera movement, lighting, environment and atmosphere, and use these details to generate a video closely matching the selected reference scene.

# Video Selection & Reproduction Case Study

**Reference Scene: Coastal Road Journey During Sunset**

The selected reference scene is a cinematic view of a modern passenger car travelling along a winding coastal road during sunset. The scene contains a curved road, car, ocean, rocky cliffs, mountains, coastal vegetation and distant buildings. The warm sunset light creates an atmospheric and peaceful travel-film appearance.

 # **Video Analysis**
 
Objects/Subjects: Passenger car, winding road, ocean, rocky cliffs, mountains, vegetation, guardrails and distant buildings.

Motion: The car moves smoothly forward along the curved coastal road while ocean waves move naturally and vegetation gently sways in the breeze.

Colors: Orange, yellow, blue, green, gray, brown and warm golden tones.

Lighting: Warm golden-hour sunlight with soft natural shadows and reflections on the car, road and ocean.

Texture: Detailed road surface, metallic car body, rocky cliffs, vegetation, ocean waves and natural mountain surfaces.

Background: Blue ocean extending toward the horizon, rocky coastline, distant mountains and small coastal buildings.

Composition: The car is positioned on a curved road in the foreground, while the ocean and mountains create depth in the background.

Camera: Smooth cinematic tracking shot following the car from a slightly elevated rear-side angle.

Style: Photorealistic cinematic travel and nature photography style.

Mood: Peaceful, adventurous, scenic and relaxing.

# Prompt Refinement Progression
**Iteration 1: Basic Prompt**

A car driving on a coastal road during sunset.

**Iteration 2: Detailed Prompt**

A modern passenger car driving slowly along a winding coastal road during sunset, with a blue ocean, rocky cliffs, green vegetation and distant mountains in the background. Warm golden sunlight illuminates the road and car, creating a peaceful cinematic travel atmosphere.

**Iteration 3: Final Fine-Tuned Prompt**

Photorealistic cinematic tracking shot of a modern passenger car travelling smoothly along a winding coastal road during golden hour. The car moves forward around a gentle curve while the blue ocean extends along the rocky coastline on the right side. Tall coastal cliffs, green vegetation, roadside bushes, guardrails and distant mountains create depth in the background. Warm orange and golden sunlight reflects naturally on the metallic surface of the car, road and ocean. Ocean waves move gently against the rocks while vegetation sways slightly in the coastal breeze. The camera follows the car from a slightly elevated rear-side angle and gradually moves forward along the curved road, revealing more of the coastline and distant mountains. Natural motion blur, realistic vehicle movement, detailed road and rock textures, atmospheric depth, realistic shadows, soft golden lighting, cinematic composition, smooth camera movement and high-quality photorealistic visuals.

# **Deliverables**

Prompt Specificity Matters: A basic prompt produces only a general car-and-coast scene, whereas a detailed prompt provides greater control over the vehicle, road, ocean, mountains, lighting and environment.
Motion Description Matters: Video prompts should describe the movement of the main subject as well as environmental elements such as ocean waves and moving vegetation.
Camera Movement: Specifying a smooth tracking shot from a slightly elevated rear-side angle helps create a realistic cinematic travel-video appearance.
Lighting and Atmosphere: Describing warm golden-hour sunlight, natural shadows and atmospheric depth improves the visual quality and realism of the generated scene.
Environmental Details: Including the winding road, rocky cliffs, ocean, mountains, vegetation and guardrails makes the generated scene more visually similar to the reference.
Temporal Progression: Describing how the camera follows the moving car and gradually reveals more of the coastline and mountains helps maintain continuity throughout the video.
Iterative Refinement: Adding specific details such as the curved road, ocean waves, sunset reflections, rocky cliffs, vehicle movement and camera direction improves the generated output compared with the basic prompt.

# Conclusion

By analyzing the visual and temporal characteristics of the selected coastal-road reference scene, a detailed text-to-video prompt was developed. The experiment demonstrates that effective video prompting requires descriptions of not only the main subject but also its movement, camera direction, environment, lighting, colors, textures and atmospheric effects. Progressive prompt refinement resulted in a more realistic and visually consistent coastal road journey video that closely represents the intended scene.

# **Result**

A 5-second photorealistic cinematic coastal-road journey video was generated using progressive prompt refinement. The final prompt successfully described the car, coastal environment, road movement, camera motion, lighting, colors, textures and atmospheric effects, resulting in a visually consistent cinematic scene.
